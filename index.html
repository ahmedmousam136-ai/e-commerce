Our Story

Where Objects
Find Meaning.

Maison Volta was founded on a single conviction: that the objects we choose to live with are a form of autobiography. Every piece in our collection is selected for its capacity to endure — in craft, in relevance, and in beauty.

We work directly with artisans and independent designers across twelve countries. No middlemen, no compromise. Just extraordinary objects at prices that respect the intelligence of our customers.

A
What Guides Us
Our Values
01
Craft First

Every object must evidence the hand that made it. We reject the anonymous and the mass-produced in favour of the deliberate and the human.

02
Radical Transparency

We publish our cost structures, supplier relationships, and margins. You deserve to know what you are buying and from whom.

03
Enduring Beauty

We do not trade in trends. Each piece is selected to gain rather than lose meaning over time — objects worthy of inheritance.

Timeline
How We Began
2021
Founded in Paris

Two former luxury buyers launch Maison Volta from a studio apartment near Oberkampf with six products and a strong conviction.

2022
First 100 Artisans

We establish direct relationships with craftspeople across France, Italy, Japan, and Morocco. Our supplier directory goes live.

2023
Global Shipping

Maison Volta begins shipping to 80 countries with carbon-neutral delivery. Revenue reaches €4M. We hire our first Tokyo-based buyer.

2025
130+ Countries

Operating from Paris, Tokyo, and New York with over 400 curated objects. Our Journal launches, celebrating each maker we represent.
