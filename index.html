Our Story
Where Objects
Find Meaning.

Maison Volta was founded on a single conviction: that the objects we choose to live with are a form of autobiography. Every piece in our collection is selected for its capacity to endure — in craft, in relevance, and in beauty.

We work directly with artisans and independent designers across twelve countries. No middlemen, no compromise. Just extraordinary objects at prices that respect the intelligence of our customers.

What Guides Us
Our Values
01
Craft First
Every object must evidence the hand that made it. We reject the machine-perfect in favour of the beautifully considered. Evidence of process is evidence of care.
02
Radical Transparency
We publish our cost structures, our supplier relationships, and our margin. Luxury should not require mystification — it should reward scrutiny.
03
Enduring Beauty
We do not trade in trends. Each piece is selected to gain rather than lose meaning over time. The only question we ask: will this still matter in twenty years?
Timeline
How We Began
2021
Founded in Paris
Two former luxury buyers, tired of the industry's opacity, launch Maison Volta from a studio apartment near Oberkampf with six products and a strong conviction.
2022
First 100 Artisans
We establish direct relationships with craftspeople across France, Italy, Japan, and Morocco. Our supplier directory goes live — the first of its kind in the sector.
2023
Global Shipping
Maison Volta begins shipping to 80 countries with carbon-neutral delivery. Revenue reaches €4M. We hire our first full-time buyer based in Tokyo.
2025
130+ Countries
Operating from Paris, Tokyo, and New York with over 400 curated objects. Our Journal launches, celebrating the stories behind each maker we represent.
